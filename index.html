Professional Profile

Geospatial data, information management, and evidence for programme delivery

I am a geospatial data and information management professional with more than 13 years of experience working across humanitarian operations, geospatial analysis, database administration, and data-driven programme support in Ethiopia. My work combines spatial and non-spatial data management, quality assurance, GIS and remote sensing, data visualization, and the development of practical information products for operational and management decisions.

In my current role with IOM–UN Migration, supporting the Shelter, Land and Site Coordination / CCCM coordination context, I help turn partner and site-level information into structured datasets, maps, dashboards, monitoring outputs, and reporting products. This includes maintaining operational datasets, checking data quality, supporting common reporting processes, and coordinating with humanitarian partners and government counterparts to improve the usefulness and consistency of shared information.

Alongside this work, I contribute to geospatial data integrity and plot verification for coffee supply-chain information under the EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR). This work strengthens my applied experience in spatial data validation, farm plot geometry, attribute review, and the use of geospatial evidence for environmental due diligence. My academic background in Remote Sensing and Geoinformatics also includes research on flood-hazard forecasting for small basins.

I am particularly interested in applying reliable geospatial data systems, remote sensing, monitoring frameworks, and accessible data products to landscape restoration, land-use monitoring, climate resilience, and evidence-based development. I bring a practical, collaborative approach: understand the information need, establish clear data rules, validate the inputs, analyse the results, and communicate findings in a form that people can use.
